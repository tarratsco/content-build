# Help with the burial flag application

Find answers to common questions below. If you need additional help, see the
[contact information at the bottom of this page](#get-help).

---

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can request a burial flag?

The following people can request a United States burial flag:

- **Next-of-kin** (surviving spouse, child, parent, brother, sister, or
  other close relative of the Veteran)
- **Funeral director or funeral home representative**
- **Veterans Service Organization (VSO) representative**
- **A close friend** when there is no living next-of-kin

Only one flag can be issued for each deceased Veteran.

---

### Does the Veteran have to be buried in a national cemetery?

No. A burial flag can be issued for a Veteran buried in any cemetery — public,
private, or religious — or interred in any other location, including at sea.
The burial does not have to take place at a VA national cemetery or state
veterans cemetery.

---

### What if the Veteran served in the National Guard or Reserves?

Veterans who served in the Selected Reserve (which includes all National Guard
and Reserve components) are eligible if they meet **at least one** of these
additional criteria:

- Was entitled to retired pay for reserve service, or would have been entitled
  but was under age 60
- Served at least one full enlistment period (or, as an officer, completed the
  initial obligation period)
- Was discharged because of a disability incurred or made worse in the line of
  duty
- Died while still a member of the Selected Reserve

If you are not sure whether the Veteran meets these criteria, you can still
submit the application. VA will make the final eligibility determination.

---

### What if the Veteran had a discharge other than honorable?

Veterans must have been discharged under conditions **other than dishonorable**
to be eligible. This includes honorable, general (under honorable conditions),
and other-than-honorable discharges — but **not** dishonorable discharges.

If you are unsure of the discharge character, you can still submit the
application. VA will review the records and make the final determination.

If you believe the Veteran's discharge character should be reviewed or upgraded,
contact your nearest VA regional office.
[Find your nearest VA regional office](/find-locations/)

---

### What documents do I need to submit with this application?

The most common document is the **DD Form 214** (Certificate of Release or
Discharge from Active Duty). For older Veterans, a WD AGO 53-55 or another
official military discharge document may be used.

You can upload a PDF, JPG, or PNG photo of the document. Maximum file size is
20 MB per file.

---

### What if I don't have the Veteran's DD Form 214?

You can still submit the application. In the Remarks section, explain that
documentation is not available and describe how you know the deceased was a
Veteran who meets the eligibility criteria.

You can also request the Veteran's military records from the **National
Personnel Records Center (NPRC)**:

- **Online:** [archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records)
- **Phone:** 1-86-NARA-NARA (1-866-272-6272)

For urgent requests related to burial, NPRC can often process requests faster
than routine requests.

---

### Do I need a VA.gov account to submit the online form?

No. You can submit the online form without signing in. This makes it easy for
funeral directors and other authorized representatives to submit on behalf of
the family.

If you sign in with Login.gov or ID.me, you can save your application progress
and may have some information pre-filled from VA records.

---

### How will I know if my application was received?

After you submit the online form, you will see a **confirmation number**
immediately on screen. If you provide an email address, you will also receive
a confirmation email through VA's notification system (VANotify).

If you mail a paper form, there is currently no automatic confirmation. Contact
the NCA Field Programs Evidence Intake Center at the number below if you need
to confirm receipt.

---

### How long does it take to process the application?

VA's service standard for complete burial benefit applications is **2 business
days**. Applications that are missing documentation or information may take
longer while VA follows up.

To avoid delays, make sure to:

- Upload the Veteran's DD Form 214 or other discharge documentation
- Fill in all required fields completely
- Explain any missing documentation in the Remarks section

---

### Can only one flag be issued?

Yes. Only one burial flag can be issued for each deceased Veteran. If the flag
was lost, stolen, or destroyed after issuance, a replacement flag may be
available. Replacement flag requests are handled separately from initial burial
flag requests and may take longer to process. Contact your nearest VA regional
office for more information about replacement flags.

---

### Can I get a burial flag if the Veteran was cremated?

Yes. A burial flag can be issued when the Veteran's remains are cremated. Enter
the location where the ashes will be interred (such as a columbarium or cemetery)
in the Place of Burial field. If there is no formal interment location, describe
the arrangement in the Remarks section.

---

### What happens to the flag after the funeral?

After the funeral or memorial service, the flag is given to the person listed
as the flag recipient on the application — usually the next-of-kin. The flag
is theirs to keep. A separate page of flag folding and use instructions is
included with the form.

---

### Can a funeral director submit this form on behalf of the family?

Yes. Funeral directors regularly submit VA Form 27-2008 on behalf of Veterans'
families. On the online form, select **"I am a funeral director or funeral home
representative"** as your applicant type. You do not need a VA.gov account to
submit.

---

### I'm a VSO representative helping a Veteran's family. Can I submit this form?

Yes. Select **"I am a Veterans Service Organization (VSO) representative or
other authorized representative"** as your applicant type on the online form.

---

### Is there a paper version of the form?

Yes. You can download the paper form at:
[VA Form 27-2008 (PDF)](https://www.va.gov/find-forms/about-form-27-2008/)

Paper forms can also be picked up at US Post Offices, VA regional offices, VA
medical centers, and most funeral homes.

Mail the completed paper form to:

> NCA Field Programs Evidence Intake Center
> P.O. Box 5237
> Janesville, WI 53547

---

### What if I started the online form but didn't finish?

If you signed in before starting the form, your progress is saved automatically.
Sign in again and return to the form to pick up where you left off.

If you did not sign in, your progress is **not saved**. The form takes about
15 minutes to complete. We recommend setting aside enough time to finish in
one session if you are not signed in.

---

### Is the online form accessible for screen readers and assistive technology?

Yes. The online form meets WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards and is fully
compatible with screen readers and other assistive technology. If you
experience any accessibility issues, please contact us using the information
below.

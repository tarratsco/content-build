# Request a Government Headstone or Marker for an Active Duty or Guard/Reserve Service Member

VA Form 40-1330M

The U.S. government provides free headstones and grave markers for active duty service members and qualifying National Guard and Reserve members who are buried in private (non-national) cemeteries. This form lets you submit that request online.

**Note:** If the service member was discharged from the military before they died, use [VA Form 40-1330](/find-forms/about-form-40-1330/) instead.

---

## Who can use this form

You can use this form if you are:

- A **next of kin** (spouse, parent, child, sibling, or other family member) of the deceased service member
- A **funeral home director or staff member** submitting on behalf of next of kin
- A **cemetery official** submitting on behalf of next of kin
- An **authorized personal representative** with written documentation

---

## Who this benefit is for

The service member must have:

- Died while on **active duty** in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, or Coast Guard, **or**
- Been a **National Guard or Reserve member** who:
  - Died in the line of duty while on active duty for training, **or**
  - Died in the line of duty while on inactive duty for training, **or**
  - Was entitled to retired pay at the time of death

The service member must also be buried in a **private cemetery** (not a national cemetery or state veterans cemetery managed by the VA).

---

## What this benefit provides

The government will furnish one of the following at no charge:

- Upright marble headstone
- Upright granite headstone
- Flat granite grave marker
- Flat marble grave marker
- Flat bronze grave marker

All headstones and markers include standard inscription. You may also request one NCA-approved emblem of belief and a brief personal inscription (character limits apply).

**Note:** The government provides and ships the headstone or marker to the cemetery. Installation is the cemetery's responsibility.

---

## What you'll need to complete this form

Gather these items before you start. You can save your progress and return later if you need time to collect documents.

### For all submissions

- **Your contact information** — name, mailing address, phone number, and email address
- **The service member's information** — legal name, Social Security number, date of birth, date of death, and branch of service
- **Cemetery information** — full name and mailing address of the private cemetery, contact person name and phone number, and grave location (section, lot, and grave number if known)
- **Official death certificate** — a clear, legible copy in PDF, JPG, or PNG format

### If the service member died on active duty

- **DD Form 1300 (Report of Casualty)** — your Casualty Assistance Officer (CAO) can provide this

### If the service member was a National Guard or Reserve member

- **NGB Form 22 or equivalent service record** — contact your state's Adjutant General office (National Guard) or the Reserve unit for a copy

### If you are not the next of kin

- **Written authorization document** showing you have the right to submit this request

---

## How long this takes

Most people complete this form in **30 to 60 minutes**, not counting time to gather documents. You can save your progress at any point and return to finish later.

---

## Before you start

You'll need to **sign in** to VA.gov to submit this form. Signing in lets you save your progress, receive a confirmation with your request number, and keep a record of your submission.

If you can't sign in or prefer to submit by mail, you can [download VA Form 40-1330M (PDF)](/find-forms/about-form-40-1330m/) and mail it to NCA Memorial Products Service.

## Privacy Act information

The information you provide on this form is covered under the Privacy Act of 1974. We will use it to process your request for a government-furnished headstone or marker. Providing your information is voluntary, but we cannot process your request without it.

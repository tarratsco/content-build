# Help with VA Form 40-1330M

**Headstone and Marker Request — Active Duty / National Guard / Reserve**

Find answers to common questions below. If you need more help, contact us using the information at the bottom of this page.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### Eligibility

#### Who can request a government headstone or marker using this form?

You can use VA Form 40-1330M if the service member died while on **active duty** or if they were a **National Guard or Reserve member** who:

- Died in the line of duty while on active duty for training
- Died in the line of duty while on inactive duty for training
- Was entitled to retired pay at the time of death

The service member must be buried in a **private cemetery** — not a national cemetery or a state veterans cemetery that provides its own markers.

Family members (next of kin), funeral home directors, cemetery officials, and authorized personal representatives can all submit this form.

#### What if the service member was discharged before they died?

If the service member left the military before they died, use **[VA Form 40-1330](/find-forms/about-form-40-1330/)** instead. VA Form 40-1330M is specifically for service members who died while on active duty or qualifying Guard/Reserve service.

The online form will ask you about the service member's status at the time of death and direct you to the correct form if needed.

#### Can I request a marker for a family member of an active duty service member?

VA Form 40-1330M covers the active duty service member. Contact NCA directly at **1-800-535-1117** to ask about eligibility for a dependent family member.

#### What if I'm not sure which qualifying circumstance applies for a Guard/Reserve member?

Contact NCA at **1-800-535-1117** or speak with a Veterans Service Organization (VSO) representative. VSOs provide free assistance and can help you determine eligibility.

### The online form

#### Do I need to sign in to use the online form?

Yes. You'll need to sign in with Login.gov or ID.me to submit the form online. Signing in lets you:

- Save your progress and return later
- Receive a confirmation with your request number
- Keep a record of your submission

If you have trouble signing in, call us at **1-800-535-1117**.

#### Can I save my progress and come back later?

Yes. You can save your progress at any point in the form by selecting "Save and finish later." Your information will be saved to your VA.gov account. Sign back in to pick up where you left off.

This is especially helpful when you're waiting to receive a copy of the DD Form 1300 or death certificate.

#### Can I submit by paper instead?

Yes. The paper form is still available. [Download VA Form 40-1330M (PDF)](/find-forms/about-form-40-1330m/) and mail or fax it to NCA Memorial Products Service. The online form and paper form are both accepted.

#### What happens after I submit?

After you submit, you'll see a confirmation page with your request number. Save or print that page for your records.

NCA Memorial Products Service will review your request. If they need more information, they'll contact you at the phone number or email address you provided. Once your request is approved and complete, NCA will place a fabrication order for the headstone or marker.

Processing time from complete submission to order placement is generally 30 days. Delivery to the cemetery takes additional time after fabrication.

#### Can I check the status of my request online?

Online status tracking is not yet available. To check the status of your request, call NCA at **1-800-535-1117**, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. ET. Have your request number ready.

### Required documents

#### Where do I get DD Form 1300 (Report of Casualty)?

Your **Casualty Assistance Officer (CAO)** — assigned by the service branch after the service member's death — can provide a copy of DD Form 1300. Contact the service branch casualty affairs office if you do not have a CAO assigned.

#### Where do I get NGB Form 22?

For **National Guard** members, contact your **state's Adjutant General office**.
For **Reserve** members, contact the **Reserve unit** where the service member served.

#### What if I only have a paper copy of the death certificate?

You can photograph a paper death certificate with your phone or scan it. Make sure:

- All text is legible
- The entire document is visible in the image
- The file is in PDF, JPG, or PNG format

If you can't upload a digital copy, you can submit the paper form by mail instead.

#### What file formats are accepted for document uploads?

Accepted formats: **PDF, JPG, PNG**
Maximum file size: Check the upload screen for the current size limit.

If your file is too large, try scanning it at a lower resolution or compressing the PDF.

#### Do I need to send original documents?

No. Copies are accepted for the online form. Do not mail original documents if you're submitting online.

### Headstone and marker selection

#### What types of headstones and markers are available?

The government currently provides:

- Upright marble headstone
- Upright granite headstone
- Flat granite grave marker
- Flat marble grave marker
- Flat bronze grave marker

All types are provided and shipped at no charge. Contact NCA at **1-800-535-1117** for the most current availability information.

#### What is inscribed on the headstone or marker?

Standard inscription includes the service member's name, rank, branch of service, and dates of birth and death. You may also request:

- One NCA-approved emblem of belief
- A brief personal inscription (character limits apply — see the form for current limits)

The inscription must match the service member's official military service records.

#### What emblems of belief are available?

NCA maintains an approved list of emblems of belief. You can select one from the list when you complete the form. If the emblem you want is not on the approved list, contact NCA before submitting — requesting an unavailable emblem will delay processing.

[View the complete NCA approved emblems of belief list](/burials-memorials/memorial-items/headstones-markers-medallions/)

#### Does the government install the headstone or marker?

No. The government provides the headstone or marker and ships it directly to the cemetery at no charge. **Installation is the cemetery's responsibility.** Contact the cemetery directly to arrange installation.

#### Can I request a government medallion instead of a headstone or marker?

If a privately purchased headstone or marker is already at the grave, you may be eligible for a **government medallion** to attach to it. The medallion program is a separate benefit. Learn more at [va.gov/burials-memorials/memorial-items/headstones-markers-medallions/](/burials-memorials/memorial-items/headstones-markers-medallions/).

### Funeral homes and cemetery officials

#### Can funeral home directors submit this form?

Funeral home directors can submit the form on behalf of next of kin. You'll need to:

- Select "Funeral home director or staff member" as your role
- Provide your funeral home's name and contact information
- Upload documentation showing you have authorization to submit on behalf of the next of kin

**Note:** A digital representative access pathway for funeral home businesses is not yet available. Funeral homes submitting on their own account should continue using the paper form until this capability is available.

#### What authorization document do I need?

A letter or signed form from the next of kin stating that you have permission to submit the headstone/marker request on their behalf is acceptable. Contact NCA at **1-800-535-1117** if you have questions about what documentation is acceptable.

## Get help

### Call NCA Memorial Products Service

**Phone:** 1-800-535-1117
**Hours:** Monday – Friday, 8:00 a.m. – 5:30 p.m. ET

### Get free help from a VSO

Veterans Service Organizations (VSOs) provide free assistance to Veterans and their families. A VSO representative can help you determine eligibility, gather documents, and complete the form.

- [Disabled American Veterans (DAV)](https://www.dav.org/)
- [Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW)](https://www.vfw.org/)
- [American Legion](https://www.legion.org/)
- [AMVETS](https://amvets.org/)

[Find a VSO accredited representative near you](/get-help-from-accredited-representative/)

### Technical help with VA.gov

If you're having trouble signing in or using the online form, contact the **VA.gov Help Desk**:

**Phone:** 1-800-698-2411 (TTY: 711)
**Hours:** Monday – Friday, 8:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m. ET

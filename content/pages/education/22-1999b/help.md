# Help with VA Form 22-1999b — Enrollment Change Certification

Find answers to common questions about this form. If you need more help, contact your VA Education Liaison Representative (ELR) or call VA's SCO support line.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is required to submit this form?

**School Certifying Officials (SCOs)** at VA-approved educational institutions are required to submit this form. Students and Veterans cannot submit this form on their behalf.

If you are a student and you believe your enrollment status has changed, contact your school's SCO immediately so they can report the change to VA.

---

### When do I need to submit this form?

You must submit VA Form 22-1999b **within 30 days** of the date the enrollment change took effect at your institution. This is a VA standard reporting requirement.

Submitting late can cause your student to receive benefit payments they are not entitled to, which creates an overpayment debt VA will require them to repay. The later you report, the larger the potential overpayment.

---

### What counts as an enrollment change I need to report?

You need to report any of the following:

- A student drops one or more courses but stays enrolled in others
- A student reduces their total credit hours
- A student ends their enrollment completely before the term ends
- A student is academically dismissed or suspended
- A student is called to active military duty and leaves school
- A student withdraws for medical or personal reasons
- You discover an error in a previously submitted VA Form 22-1999

If you are unsure whether a specific situation requires a report, contact your VA Education Liaison Representative before the 30-day window closes.

---

### What is a VA Facility Code and where do I find mine?

Your **VA Facility Code** is an 8-digit number that VA assigns to your school location. It identifies your specific campus in VA's education records.

You can find your Facility Code:

- In your VAONCE account
- On a previously submitted VA Form 22-1999
- By contacting your VA Education Liaison Representative

If your school has multiple campuses, each campus location has its own Facility Code. Use the Facility Code for the specific campus where the student is enrolled.

---

### What is the "last date of attendance" and why does VA need it?

The **last date of attendance** is the last calendar date a student attended class or participated in a documented academic activity at your institution. This is not the same as the official withdrawal date.

VA uses this date to calculate when to stop or reduce the student's housing allowance (for Post-9/11 GI Bill students) and other payments. Providing an accurate last date of attendance helps VA calculate the correct overpayment amount — or avoid establishing an overpayment at all.

For **online courses**, use the last date the student completed a documented academic activity — not their last login date.

For **in-person courses**, use the last date they were physically present in class, based on your institution's attendance records.

---

### What are mitigating circumstances and why should I document them?

**Mitigating circumstances** are situations beyond a student's control that contributed to their withdrawal or enrollment change. Examples include:

- A serious illness or injury
- A death or serious illness in the student's immediate family
- A military deployment or activation order
- A natural disaster affecting the student's ability to attend

Under federal law (38 U.S.C. § 3699), VA is required to consider documented mitigating circumstances before establishing an overpayment debt against a student. If you document mitigating circumstances in this form, VA may reduce or eliminate the student's overpayment.

**You are not required to investigate circumstances.** Report only what you know from your institution's records or direct communications with the student. Even partial information is helpful.

---

### What if I am reporting a change more than 30 days after it happened?

You can still submit this form even if the 30-day reporting window has passed. This form will ask you to provide a brief explanation of why the report is late.

Late reporting does not prevent submission, but it may result in a larger overpayment for your student because VA will need to recover payments made during the unreported period.

If you regularly experience difficulty meeting the 30-day reporting window, contact your VA Education Liaison Representative to discuss your school's reporting processes.

---

### Can I submit this form without signing in to VA.gov?

No. You must be signed in to VA.gov as an authenticated School Certifying Official to use this digital form. Authentication helps VA confirm your identity and your institution affiliation, and it allows the form to pre-fill your institution information to reduce data entry.

If you are unable to sign in, contact your VA Education Liaison Representative for help. You may also use VAONCE to submit enrollment change certifications electronically while this digital form is in development.

---

### Will the student be notified when I submit this form?

The student will not receive an automatic notification from VA when you submit this form. However, VA will contact the student separately if their benefit payments change or if an overpayment is established.

We recommend that you inform the student directly when you submit an enrollment change certification on their behalf, so they are aware that a change is being reported and can expect possible adjustments to their payments.

---

### I submitted this form but made an error. What do I do?

If you realize you made an error after submitting, submit a new VA Form 22-1999b and select **"Correction to a previously submitted certification"** as the type of change. Explain what was incorrect and provide the corrected information.

Do not resubmit the original change type — use the correction option to avoid creating conflicting records.

---

### How do I know VA received my submission?

After you submit this form, you will see a confirmation page with:

- A unique confirmation number
- The date and time of your submission
- A summary of the key information you submitted

You will also receive a confirmation email at the email address you entered in the form. Save or print this confirmation for your records.

If you do not receive a confirmation email within 1 business day, check your spam folder. If you still do not receive a confirmation, call VA's SCO support line at 1-888-442-4551.

---

### What is the difference between this form and VA Form 22-1999?

| Form | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **VA Form 22-1999 — Enrollment Certification** | Certifies a student's initial enrollment in a term or period of training |
| **VA Form 22-1999b — Enrollment Change Certification** | Reports changes, reductions, or terminations to an enrollment that has already been certified |

You submit a 22-1999 at the beginning of each enrollment period. You submit a 22-1999b any time something changes during that enrollment period.

---

### Which GI Bill chapters does this form cover?

This digital form covers the following benefit chapters:

- **Chapter 33** — Post-9/11 GI Bill
- **Chapter 30** — Montgomery GI Bill — Active Duty (MGIB-AD)
- **Chapter 35** — Survivors' and Dependents' Educational Assistance
- **Chapter 1606** — Montgomery GI Bill — Selected Reserve

Support for additional benefit programs may be added in future versions of this form.

## Get more help

### Call VA's School Certifying Official support line

**1-888-442-4551**  
Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. ET  
TTY: 711

This line is for School Certifying Officials only. If you are a student, call the VA education benefits line at 1-888-442-4551 (same number — ask for student benefits).

### Contact your VA Education Liaison Representative (ELR)

Your ELR is your primary VA contact for questions about GI Bill enrollment certification, form requirements, and reporting procedures.

[Find your VA Education Liaison Representative](https://www.benefits.va.gov/gibill/resources/education_resources/school_certifying_officials/elr.asp)

### Use the VA Ask a Question tool

[Ask VA a question online](https://ask.va.gov)  
Select **"Education benefits and work study"** as your topic.

### Send a secure message through GI Bill School Portal

If your institution uses the GI Bill School Portal, you can submit questions through the portal's messaging function.

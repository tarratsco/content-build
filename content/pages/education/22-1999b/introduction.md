# Report an enrollment change for a student using GI Bill benefits (VA Form 22-1999b)

Use this form to report a change to a student's enrollment after you have already submitted their original enrollment certification (VA Form 22-1999). Submit this form as soon as a change occurs — within 30 days of the effective date of the change.

**Important:** If you do not report enrollment changes on time, your student may receive benefit payments they are not entitled to. This can result in an overpayment debt that VA will require them to repay.

---

## Who can use this form

This form is for **School Certifying Officials (SCOs)** at VA-approved educational institutions. You must be signed in to VA.gov as an authenticated SCO to use this form.

If you are a student-Veteran and you believe your school has not reported a change in your enrollment, contact your SCO directly or call VA at 1-888-442-4551.

---

## What this form is for

Use VA Form 22-1999b to report any of the following changes after an enrollment certification has been processed:

- A student reduced their credit hours but is still enrolled
- A student withdrew from one or more courses but is still enrolled in others
- A student ended their enrollment completely (full termination)
- You need to correct an error on a previously submitted VA Form 22-1999

**Do not use this form** to certify a new enrollment period. Use VA Form 22-1999 for initial enrollment certifications.

---

## GI Bill chapters covered by this form

This form covers enrollment changes for students receiving benefits under:

- **Chapter 33** — Post-9/11 GI Bill
- **Chapter 30** — Montgomery GI Bill — Active Duty (MGIB-AD)
- **Chapter 35** — Survivors' and Dependents' Educational Assistance
- **Chapter 1606** — Montgomery GI Bill — Selected Reserve

---

## What you'll need to complete this form

Have the following information ready before you start:

**Your information (as the SCO):**
- Your VA Facility Code (8-digit code assigned to your institution)
- Your name, title, phone number, and email address

**Student information:**
- Student's full legal name
- Student's Social Security number (SSN) or VA File Number
- GI Bill benefit chapter under which the student is certified

**Original certification information:**
- Begin and end dates from the original VA Form 22-1999 you are amending
- Number of credit hours originally certified
- Original enrollment status (full-time, three-quarter time, half-time, or less than half-time)

**Change details:**
- Type of enrollment change (reduction, withdrawal, termination, or correction)
- Effective date of the change
- Last date of attendance (required for withdrawals and terminations)
- Reason for the change
- Any mitigating circumstances you are aware of

**Supporting documents (if applicable):**
- Official withdrawal notice
- Medical documentation (if the student withdrew due to illness)
- Deployment orders (if applicable)
- Correction documentation from your registrar (for correction submissions)

---

## How long this takes

**Estimated completion time:** 10–20 minutes, depending on the type of change you are reporting. Corrections and submissions with supporting documents may take longer.

---

## After you submit

After you submit this form, you will receive:

- A **confirmation number** and **timestamp** on screen
- A **confirmation email** sent to the email address you provide

Your confirmation means VA has received your submission. It does not mean VA has finished processing the change or that the student's benefit payments have been adjusted yet.

VA will review your submission and update the student's benefit payments. This process typically takes up to 21 days for electronic submissions. The student will receive a separate notice from VA about any changes to their payments or any overpayment determination.

# Help with VA Form 10-2850D

## Frequently asked questions

---

### About the form

**What is VA Form 10-2850D?**

VA Form 10-2850D is the Health Professions Trainee Data Collection
Form. VA requires this form from all health professions trainees
before they can begin any VA-affiliated clinical training program.
The form collects your personal information, citizenship status,
license and credential history, education and training history,
and responses to required suitability questions.

---

**Who needs to complete this form?**

You need to complete this form if you are entering a VA-affiliated
clinical training program as a health professions trainee. This
includes medical residents, nursing students, pharmacy trainees,
psychology interns, social work interns, dentistry trainees, and
allied health trainees.

This form is not for Veterans applying for VA health care or
benefits. It is only for health professions trainees seeking
clinical training appointments.

---

**I usually download and print the PDF form. Can I still do that?**

Yes. The fillable PDF version of VA Form 10-2850D is still
available at [va.gov/vaforms/medical/pdf/VA-10-2850d-fill.pdf](https://www.va.gov/vaforms/medical/pdf/VA-10-2850d-fill.pdf)
while the online form is available. Talk to your VA training
facility's education coordinator about which method they prefer.

---

**Is the online version of this form official?**

Yes. The online form collects the same information as the paper
form and meets the same legal requirements, including both
required electronic signatures. The online form is covered by
OMB Control Number 2900-0205.

---

### Signing in

**Why do I have to sign in to complete this form?**

This form asks for sensitive personal information, including your
Social Security number, clinical license history, immigration
status, and authorization for VA to contact your employers and
licensing boards. Signing in protects your information and lets
you save your progress if you need to return later.

---

**I am not a Veteran. Can I still create a Login.gov account?**

Yes. Login.gov accounts are available to anyone, not just
Veterans. You can create an account at
[login.gov/create-an-account/](https://login.gov/create-an-account/).
You will need to verify your identity when you create your
account. Have a government-issued photo ID ready.

---

**I am an international trainee on a J-1 visa. Can I create a
Login.gov account?**

Login.gov requires identity verification. International trainees
who do not have a U.S. driver's license or state-issued ID may
have difficulty completing the identity verification step online.
If you are unable to verify your identity online, you may
complete identity verification in person at a U.S. Post Office
that participates in the Login.gov in-person proofing program.
Visit [login.gov/help/verify-your-identity/verify-your-identity-in-person/](https://login.gov/help/verify-your-identity/verify-your-identity-in-person/)
for more information.

If you are unable to complete identity verification, contact
your VA training facility's education coordinator for help.

---

**I started the form but my session timed out. Did I lose my work?**

If you were signed in when you started the form, your progress
was saved automatically. Sign back in and your in-progress
application will be waiting for you. If you were not signed in,
you may have lost your progress. We recommend signing in before
you begin.

---

### Completing the form

**How long does it take to complete this form?**

The estimated completion time is 30 minutes. This estimate
assumes you have all of your license, education, and training
information ready before you begin. If you need to look up
license numbers, expiration dates, ECFMG certificate information,
or training dates, it may take longer. You can save your progress
and return at any time.

---

**What if I do not have a current license or certification?**

Not all health professions trainees hold a professional license.
You are only required to list licenses, certifications, and
registrations that you actually hold. If you have no current
licenses, you can proceed without adding any entries in the
current licenses section.

---

**What is an ECFMG certificate? Do I need one?**

The Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG)
certificate is required for graduates of international medical
schools who are completing graduate medical education (residency
or fellowship) in the United States. If you attended a medical
school outside the United States and Canada, you will need to
enter your ECFMG certificate number and date.

If you did not attend an international medical school, you do not
need an ECFMG certificate and can answer "No" to the international
medical school question.

---

**What is a DS-2019? Do I need to complete that section?**

A DS-2019 (Certificate of Eligibility for Exchange Visitor Status)
is issued by your sponsoring organization and is required for
J-1 Exchange Visitor trainees. If you are a J-1 Exchange Visitor,
you will need to answer the DS-2019 question and provide the date
of your last DS-2019 validation.

If you are not a J-1 Exchange Visitor, answer "No" to the
DS-2019 question.

---

**What is a National Provider Identifier (NPI)?**

An NPI is a 10-digit number issued by the Centers for Medicare
and Medicaid Services (CMS) to identify health care providers.
Not all trainees are required to have one. If you have an NPI,
enter it in the form. If you do not have one, leave the field
blank. You can look up your NPI at the CMS National Plan and
Provider Enumeration System (NPPES) registry at
[nppes.cms.hhs.gov](https://nppes.cms.hhs.gov).

---

**I answered "Yes" to a question about license actions or
malpractice. What do I need to do?**

If you answered "Yes" to any of these questions, you are required
to provide a written explanation:

- Item 16: License, certification, or registration actions
- Item 17: Clinical privilege actions
- Item 21: Medicare or Medicaid fraud conviction or investigation
- Item 22: Malpractice proceedings

After you answer "Yes," the form will ask you to write a detailed
explanation. You can also upload supporting documents. You must
complete the explanation before you can submit the form.

**Failing to provide an explanation when you answered "Yes" is
the most common reason paper applications are returned
incomplete.** The online form will not let you submit without
the required explanation.

The VA Designated Education Officer (DEO) will review your
explanation carefully. As stated in the form: "It is recognized
that many allegations of professional malpractice are proven
groundless. Any conclusion concerning your answer as it relates
to professional qualifications will be made only after a full
evaluation of the circumstances involved."

---

**I need to list more education or training history than the
form has space for. What do I do?**

The form lets you add multiple entries in the education and
training history sections. If you need to provide information
that does not fit in the available entries, use the "Additional
remarks" section at the end of the form. Begin your remark with
the item number you are continuing (for example: "Item 18:
I also attended...").

---

**What are the two signature steps at the end of the form?**

The form requires two separate electronic acknowledgments:

1. **Certification (Item 23A):** You certify that all statements
   you made in the form are true, correct, complete, and made
   in good faith. This is required by 18 U.S.C. § 1001.

2. **Authorization for Release of Information:** You authorize
   VA to contact your current and previous employers, educational
   institutions, state licensing boards, and other organizations
   to verify your credentials and background. You also authorize
   VA to share information about you with your affiliated training
   institution. This authorization has five separate clauses that
   you must each acknowledge.

Both acknowledgments are required. You cannot submit the form
without completing both.

---

**What happens to my application after I submit it?**

After you submit:

1. You will receive a confirmation page with a reference number.
   Save or print this for your records.

2. The Designated Education Officer (DEO) at your VA training
   facility will review your application and verify your
   qualifications.

3. The Medical Center Director at your facility must approve
   your appointment.

4. Your facility's education office will contact you about
   your appointment status.

If you have questions about the status of your application,
contact your VA training facility's education coordinator
directly.

---

### Technical help

**The form is not loading. What should I do?**

Try these steps:

1. Refresh the page.
2. Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then try again.
3. Try a different web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
4. Make sure your browser is up to date.
5. Check that you are connected to the internet.

If the form still does not load, call the VA at
[1-800-827-1000](tel:18008271000).

---

**I got an error message when I tried to submit. What should I do?**

If you see an error message when you submit, your application
may not have been received. Do not close the form. Note any
error message text, then try submitting again. If the error
continues, call the VA at [1-800-827-1000](tel:18008271000)
or contact your VA training facility's education coordinator.

---

**I did not receive a confirmation email after submitting. Did
my application go through?**

Check your confirmation page for a reference number. If you see
a reference number, your application was submitted successfully.
The confirmation page is your primary receipt — save or print it.

Confirmation emails are sent to the primary email address you
provided in the form. Check your spam or junk folder. If you
do not receive a confirmation email within 24 hours and you
did not save your confirmation page, contact your VA training
facility's education coordinator.

---

## Get more help

**Call VA:**
[1-800-827-1000](tel:18008271000)
Monday – Friday, 8:00 a.m. – 9:00 p.m. ET

**Contact your VA training facility:**
For questions specific to your appointment, training program,
or the status of your application, contact your VA training
facility's education coordinator directly. Your program
coordinator at your affiliated institution can provide contact
information for your facility's Designated Education Officer.

# Apply for a VA Clinical Training Appointment

## VA Form 10-2850D — Health Professions Trainee Data Collection Form

Use this form to apply for a clinical training appointment at a
VA facility. You must complete and submit this form before you
can begin any VA-affiliated clinical training program.

**OMB Control Number:** 2900-0205
**OMB Expiration Date:** 05/31/2026
**Estimated completion time:** 30 minutes

---

## Who should use this form

You should complete VA Form 10-2850D if you are a health professions
trainee applying to a VA clinical training program. This includes:

- Medical residents and interns
- Nursing students
- Pharmacy trainees
- Psychology interns
- Social work interns
- Dentistry trainees
- Allied health trainees
- Any student or trainee in a health profession entering a
  VA-affiliated training program

**You are not a Veteran applying for VA health care or benefits.**
This form is only for health professions trainees seeking clinical
training appointments at VA facilities.

---

## What to know before you start

### You will need to sign in

You must sign in with a Login.gov account to complete this form.
Signing in lets you:

- Save your progress and return later
- Get a confirmation of your submission
- Allow the VA to securely receive your personal and professional
  information

If you do not have a Login.gov account, you can create one before
you start. [Create a Login.gov account](https://login.gov/create-an-account/).

### Gather your information before you start

This form asks for detailed professional and personal information.
Collecting everything before you begin will make the process faster.

**Personal information:**
- Your full legal name and any other names you have used
  (maiden names, prior legal names, names used on licenses)
- Your Social Security number
- Your date of birth
- Your current mailing address, phone number, and email address
- The name and address of your VA training facility and your
  training start and end dates

**Military status (if applicable):**
- Whether you are on active duty, in the Reserves, or in the
  National Guard
- Your branch of service

**Citizenship and immigration documents (if you are not a
U.S. citizen):**
- Your visa type and visa number
- Visa issue and expiration dates
- Your Alien Registration Number ("A" number) if you hold an
  immigrant visa
- Your DS-2019 document and date of last validation if you are
  a J-1 Exchange Visitor

**Licenses, certifications, and registrations:**
- The name, type, and number of every current health professional
  license, certification, or registration you hold — in any state
  or jurisdiction
- The expiration date for each license, certification, or
  registration
- Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) certificate information
  if applicable
- All prior licenses, certifications, and registrations you have
  ever held, even if no longer active
- Your National Provider Identifier (NPI) number, if you have one

**Education history:**
- Names and addresses of all schools or programs you attended
  after high school
- Start and completion dates (or expected completion dates) for
  each program
- Degrees, diplomas, or certificates awarded or in progress
- Your major field of study for each program

**If you are a graduate of an international medical school:**
- Your Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG)
  certificate number and date

**Training history:**
- Names and addresses of hospitals or institutions where you
  completed or are completing internships, residencies, or
  fellowships
- Your specialty for each training program
- Start and completion dates and the number of months you completed

**Adverse history (if applicable):**
- A written explanation for any "Yes" answer to questions about
  license or privilege actions (Items 16 and 17)
- A written explanation for any "Yes" answer to questions about
  Medicare or Medicaid fraud investigations (Item 21) or
  malpractice proceedings (Item 22)
- Supporting documents you want to submit with those explanations

## What happens after you submit

After you submit this form, here is what to expect:

1. **VA reviews your application.** The Designated Education
   Officer (DEO) at your VA training facility will review your
   application and verify your qualifications against the Trainee
   Qualifications and Credentials Verification Letter (TQCVL).

2. **Medical Center Director approval.** The Medical Center
   Director at your training facility must approve your appointment
   before you can begin training.

3. **Your facility will contact you.** Your VA training facility's
   education office will reach out to you about your appointment
   status. If you have questions before then, contact your
   facility's education coordinator directly.

## Privacy and legal notices

**Privacy Act information:** The information you provide on this
form is collected under Title 38, U.S.C., and the Privacy Act of
1974. VA uses this information to determine your eligibility and
qualifications for a health professions training appointment.
Your records are maintained under VA System of Records Notice
02VA135.

**Social Security number:** Providing your Social Security number
is mandatory. The authority to request this information is Public
Law 93-579, Section 7(b), and Executive Order 9397 (November 22,
1943). VA uses your SSN to identify your federal records.

**False statements warning:** A false statement on any part of
this application may be grounds for not approving your application
or for terminating your appointment. You may also be fined or
imprisoned under 18 U.S.C. § 1001.

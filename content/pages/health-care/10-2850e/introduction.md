# Apply for a VHA Clinical Position

## VA Form 10-2850e

> **Note:** The online version of this form is in development and available
> at select VA Medical Centers (VAMCs) only. Contact the HR office at the
> VAMC where you are applying to confirm whether digital submission is
> available at that location.

Use this form to apply for a clinical healthcare professional position at a
VA Medical Center. This form starts the credentialing and privileging process
required before you can see patients at a VA facility.

---

## Who should use this form

Use VA Form 10-2850e if you are a licensed healthcare professional applying for:

- An initial clinical appointment at a VA Medical Center
- Reappointment or recredentialing at your current VA facility
- Transfer credentialing to a different VA Medical Center
- A temporary or fee-basis clinical appointment

---

## Before you start

### What you'll need to complete this form

Gather these documents and information before you begin. Having everything
ready will help you finish faster and avoid errors.

**Personal information:**
- Your Social Security number
- Your date and place of birth
- Your current home and mailing address
- Your professional email address and phone number

**Professional licenses:**
- License type, state, license number, issue date, and expiration date for
  every professional license you have ever held — including licenses in
  other states and licenses that are no longer active

**Board certifications:**
- Certifying board name, certification number, and dates for each
  board certification you hold or have held

**DEA registration (if applicable):**
- Your DEA registration number, authorized schedules, and expiration date
- A copy of your current DEA registration certificate

**Education and training:**
- Name of your degree-granting institution, degree type, and graduation date
- Program name, sponsoring institution, and dates for each residency,
  fellowship, or internship you completed

**Employment history (past 10 years):**
- Employer name and address, your position title, start and end dates, and
  reason for leaving for every position you held in the past 10 years
- Be ready to explain any gaps of 30 days or more between positions

**Professional references:**
- Name, professional title, institution, phone number, and email address for
  at least 3 professional references who can speak to your clinical work
- References must be professional colleagues or supervisors — not family
  members or personal friends

**Adverse history information (if applicable):**
- Details about any adverse actions taken against your professional license
  or clinical privileges
- Details about any malpractice claims filed against you
- Details about any criminal convictions

**Supporting documents to upload:**
- Copies of all current professional licenses
- Copy of your DEA registration certificate (if applicable)
- Copies of board certification certificates (if applicable)
- Current malpractice insurance certificate
- Supporting documentation for any adverse history disclosures

## How long this form takes

Most applicants take **60 to 90 minutes** to complete this form. The form
has 8 sections:

1. Applicant information
2. Professional licenses and certifications
3. Education and training
4. Employment history
5. Adverse history disclosure
6. Supporting document uploads
7. Appointment details
8. Attestation and electronic signature

You can **save your progress** and come back later. Sign in to VA.gov first,
and we'll save your work as you go.

## What happens after you submit

After you submit this form, the VA Medical Center's credentialing office will:

1. Review your application for completeness
2. Conduct primary source verification of your licenses and credentials
3. Query the National Practitioner Data Bank (NPDB)
4. Present your application to the Credentials Committee for review
5. Contact you and your supervisor with a decision about clinical privileges

**Important:** Submitting this form does not guarantee appointment or grant
of clinical privileges. Credentialing review must be completed before you
can see patients at a VA facility.

You will receive a confirmation with a reference number when you submit.
Keep this number so you can follow up with the VAMC HR office if needed.

## Privacy information

The information you provide on this form is protected under the Privacy Act
of 1974. VA uses this information to evaluate your application for a clinical
position and to conduct the required credentialing and privileging review.

For more information, see the
[VA Privacy Policy](https://www.va.gov/privacy-policy/).

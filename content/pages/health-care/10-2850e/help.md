# Help with VA Form 10-2850e

**VA Form 10-2850e — VHA Clinical Position Application**

Find answers to common questions below. If you need more help, contact the
VA Medical Center where you are applying or call us at 1-800-698-2411.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### About this form

**What is VA Form 10-2850e used for?**

VA Form 10-2850e is used to apply for a clinical healthcare professional
position at a VA Medical Center. It starts the credentialing and privileging
process that VA requires before any clinical provider can see patients at
a VA facility.

**Who needs to fill out this form?**

Licensed clinical healthcare professionals applying for an initial
appointment, reappointment, transfer credentialing, or a temporary
clinical appointment at a VA Medical Center should use this form.

**How is this form different from VA Form 10-2850, 10-2850a, or 10-2850c?**

The VA Form 10-2850 series covers different clinical occupational categories:

- **10-2850** — Physicians and dentists
- **10-2850a** — Nurses (RN, NP, CRNA, and related roles)
- **10-2850c** — Associated health occupations
- **10-2850e** — Specific occupational category (verify with the VAMC HR
  office which form applies to your position)

Contact the HR office at the facility where you are applying if you are
not sure which form to use.

**Do I need to create an account to use this form online?**

Yes. You must sign in with a verified Login.gov or ID.me account to use
the online form. This protects your personal information, including your
Social Security number and health credentials.

### Completing the form

**Can I save my progress and come back later?**

Yes. The online form saves your progress automatically as you go. Sign in
before you start, and you can return at any time to pick up where you left
off. Your saved application will be available for **60 days** from the
date you last worked on it.

**How long does it take to complete this form?**

Most applicants take 60 to 90 minutes. Complex applications — especially
those with extensive employment histories, multiple licenses across many
states, or adverse history disclosures — may take longer. We recommend
gathering all required documents before you start.

**What if I make a mistake on my application?**

You can edit any section of your application on the Review and Submit page
before you submit. After submission, contact the HR office at the VA
Medical Center where you applied to request a correction.

**Do I need to list licenses I held in other states, even if they expired?**

Yes. You must list every professional license you have ever held, in every
state or jurisdiction, including licenses that are inactive, expired,
surrendered, or revoked. VA's credentialing process includes primary source
verification and National Practitioner Data Bank (NPDB) queries that will
surface all license history. Omitting a license — even an old one — may
cause delays or be grounds for disqualification.

**Do I need to disclose adverse history that was expunged or that happened
a long time ago?**

Yes. You must answer adverse history questions honestly, even if:

- The matter was resolved, expunged, or sealed
- The action was taken in another state
- The event happened many years ago
- A court found in your favor

You will have a chance to explain the circumstances of any adverse event.
VA credentialing staff review context as part of the credentialing decision.
Failing to disclose a known adverse event is more serious than the event
itself.

**What if I have an employment gap of more than 30 days?**

You will be prompted to explain any gap of 30 days or more in your
employment history. Common reasons include maternity or paternity leave,
relocation, illness, continuing education, or time between positions. All
gaps must be explained — there is no gap that is automatically disqualifying.

**What do I do if a required field does not apply to me?**

- For fields that genuinely do not apply (for example, DEA registration for
  a non-prescribing role), select the "Not applicable" option where it appears.
- For optional fields, leave them blank.
- If you are unsure whether something applies to your situation, contact the
  VAMC HR office where you are applying.

**Can I apply to more than one VA Medical Center at once?**

No. You must submit a separate application for each VA Medical Center where
you want to be credentialed. Each VAMC conducts its own credentialing review.

### Electronic signature and legal questions

**Is an electronic signature on this form legally valid?**

The online form uses an electronic signature under the E-SIGN Act (15 U.S.C.
§ 7001). You sign by typing your full legal name in the signature field.

**Note:** The legal sufficiency of electronic signatures for all sections of
this form is subject to confirmation by VHA General Counsel. Some VA Medical
Centers may require a wet-ink (handwritten) signature for certain sections.
Contact the VAMC HR office where you are applying if you have questions about
signature requirements.

**Will VA share my information with other organizations?**

VA uses your information to evaluate your application and conduct the required
credentialing review. This includes:

- Querying the National Practitioner Data Bank (NPDB)
- Contacting state licensing boards to verify your licenses (primary source
  verification)
- Contacting prior employers listed on your application
- Conducting a background investigation

By submitting this form, you authorize VA to contact these sources. See the
Privacy Act statement on the introduction page for details.

### After you submit

**What happens after I submit this form?**

After you submit:

1. You will receive a confirmation page with a reference number. Save this
   number.
2. The VAMC credentialing office will review your application for completeness.
3. VA will verify your licenses, education, employment history, and references
   through primary source verification.
4. VA will query the National Practitioner Data Bank.
5. The Credentials Committee at the VAMC will review your application and
   make a privileging decision.
6. You and your hiring supervisor will be notified of the decision.

**Does submitting this form mean I'm hired or approved to see patients?**

No. Submitting this form is the first step. You cannot begin clinical duties
at a VA facility until:

- The credentialing review process is complete
- The Credentials Committee has reviewed and approved your application
- Clinical privileges have been formally granted
- All background investigation requirements have been met

**How long does credentialing review take?**

Processing times vary by facility and the complexity of your application.
Contact the HR or Medical Staff Office at the VAMC where you applied for
an estimate. Common reasons for delays include:

- Missing documents (licenses, certificates, insurance certificate)
- Unexplained employment gaps
- Adverse history disclosures that require additional documentation
- Delays in primary source verification from licensing boards

**My application was returned as incomplete. What do I do?**

Contact the HR office at the VAMC where you applied. They will tell you
which items are missing or need correction. You may need to resubmit
certain sections or provide additional documentation.

**How do I check the status of my application?**

Contact the Medical Staff Office or HR office at the VA Medical Center
where you submitted your application. Reference your submission reference
number when you call.

### Technical help

**The form isn't loading. What should I do?**

Try these steps:

1. Sign out and sign back in to VA.gov
2. Clear your browser's cache and cookies
3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
4. Disable browser extensions or try an incognito/private window
5. Check [VA.gov status](https://www.va.gov/) for any system outages

If the problem continues, call us at 1-800-698-2411 (TTY: 711).

**My file upload failed. What are the accepted formats and size limits?**

Accepted file formats: **PDF, JPG, PNG**

Maximum file size: **20 MB per file**

If your file is too large, try:
- Scanning at a lower resolution (150–200 DPI is usually sufficient)
- Saving a PDF at a compressed or reduced file size setting
- Converting a multi-page document to a smaller PDF

**I lost my saved application. What do I do?**

Saved applications are stored for 60 days from your last activity. If more
than 60 days have passed since you last worked on your application, it may
have been deleted. You would need to start a new application.

If you believe your application was lost due to a technical error, call us
at 1-800-698-2411 (TTY: 711).

**The form isn't working with my screen reader or assistive technology.**

This form is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards and VA
Section 508 requirements. If you experience accessibility barriers:

- Call us at 1-800-698-2411 (TTY: 711) to request assistance completing
  the form
- Contact the [VA Section 508 Office](https://www.section508.va.gov/) to
  report an accessibility issue

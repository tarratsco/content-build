# Help with VA Form 10-7959E

Find answers to common questions below. If you don't see your question here,
contact the VHA Office of Integrated Veteran Care (OIVC) directly.

**OIVC phone:** 1-833-930-0816
**Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:05 a.m. – 6:30 p.m. ET
**Mail:** VHA Office of Integrated Veteran Care, P.O. Box 400,
Spring City, PA 19475
**Fax:** 1-303-331-7807

## Eligibility questions

### Who can submit this form?

This form is only for beneficiaries of two specific VA health care programs:

- The **Spina Bifida Health Care Benefits Program**
- The **Children of Women Vietnam Veterans (CWVV) Program**

A parent, guardian, or other authorized representative may file on behalf
of a minor child or an incapacitated adult beneficiary.

If you are not enrolled in one of these programs, this form does not apply
to you.

### My child is the beneficiary, but I'm the one submitting. Is that okay?

Yes. A parent or guardian may complete and sign this form on behalf of a
minor child. In Section IV (Certification), you'll indicate that a
representative is signing, and you'll provide your own name, Social Security
number, address, and phone number.

### Can I file for both the Spina Bifida program and the CWVV program on
the same form?

No. You'll select one program at the start of the online form. Both programs
use the same form and the same mailing address, but each claim must identify
which program the patient is enrolled in.

## Expenses and receipts

### What expenses can I claim on this form?

You can claim reimbursement for out-of-pocket expenses directly related to
covered medical appointments, including:

- Airline, bus, train, taxi, and other transportation
- Privately owned vehicle (POV) mileage
- Lodging (hotel or other overnight accommodation)
- Meals
- Parking and tolls

You **cannot** use this form to claim the cost of medical services or
provider charges. Those are claimed through the standard OIVC claims process.

### Do I need receipts for everything?

You need receipts for all expenses **except** POV mileage. Upload photos or
PDFs of your receipts when you complete the online form. If you are
submitting by mail or fax, attach paper copies.

Without receipts for non-mileage expenses, your claim will be delayed or
denied.

### What if I drove my own car to the appointment?

You can claim privately owned vehicle (POV) mileage reimbursement without a
receipt. You'll need to enter your departure and arrival cities and states
for the trip. Reimbursement is calculated at the applicable round-trip
mileage rate set by OIVC.

To confirm the current mileage rate, call OIVC at **1-833-930-0816**.

### Are there limits on how much I can claim for lodging or meals?

Contact OIVC at **1-833-930-0816** to ask about any applicable per diem
limits for lodging and meals. Reimbursement caps may apply based on
destination city rates.

### Can I claim expenses for an attendant who traveled with my child?

Yes. If a person (other than the patient) traveled with the patient to a
covered appointment, you can claim that attendant's travel and miscellaneous
expenses on the same form. You'll need to provide the attendant's name and
relationship to the patient.

## Using the online form

### Do I need to sign in to use the online form?

Yes. You must sign in with Login.gov or ID.me at a verified identity level
(LOA2/IAL2) to submit this form online. This requirement protects your
information because the form includes Social Security numbers for both the
patient and the sponsor.

Signing in also lets us fill in some of your information automatically,
which saves you time.

### What file types can I upload for receipts?

You can upload:

- **PDF** files
- **JPG or JPEG** image files
- **PNG** image files

Each file must be **25 MB or smaller**. You can upload multiple files.

If your receipt is on paper, you can take a photo of it with your phone and
upload that photo directly.

### Can I save my progress and come back later?

Yes. If you are signed in, you can save your progress and return to finish
your claim later. Saved forms expire after 60 days.

If you leave the form without saving, your progress may be lost.

### What is the provider certification, and why do I need it?

If you are claiming travel expenses, federal rules require that a medical
provider certify the date and nature of the service. In Section III of the
form, you'll enter:

- The date of the medical appointment
- The provider's Tax ID number
- The provider's name (typed as an electronic signature, as allowed by the
  form's own instructions)

Contact your provider's office ahead of time to get the Tax ID number (also
called a TIN or EIN).

### Why is Section III only showing up for travel expenses?

The form's instructions state that Section III (Travel) is **required only
when travel expenses are claimed**. If you are only claiming lodging, meals,
or other non-travel expenses, you will skip Section III and go directly to
the expense amounts and receipt upload.

## After you submit

### How will I know VA received my claim?

When you submit the online form, you'll see a confirmation page with a
confirmation number and the date of submission. You'll also receive an email
confirmation at the address on file in your VA profile.

If you submitted by mail or fax, there is currently no online way to confirm
receipt. Call OIVC at **1-833-930-0816** to confirm your paper submission
was received.

### How long does it take to process a claim?

OIVC will review your claim after it is received. Processing times vary.
Contact OIVC at **1-833-930-0816** for the most current information on
processing timelines.

### What if OIVC needs more information?

OIVC will contact you at the phone number or mailing address you provided.
Make sure your contact information is up to date.

### Where will reimbursement be sent?

If your claim is approved, reimbursement will be issued to the beneficiary
(the patient). Contact OIVC at **1-833-930-0816** to ask about payment
method and payee setup, especially if this is your first claim.

# Claim for Miscellaneous Expenses (VA Form 10-7959E)

VA Form 10-7959E | OMB Control No. 2900-0219 | Expires 12/31/2027

Use this form to request reimbursement for out-of-pocket expenses related to
covered medical appointments. This includes travel, lodging, meals, mileage,
parking, and tolls.

**Estimated time to complete: 10 minutes**

---

## Who can use this form

This form is only for:

- Beneficiaries of the **Spina Bifida Health Care Benefits Program**
  (authorized under 38 U.S.C. §§ 1802–1805), **or**
- Beneficiaries of the **Children of Women Vietnam Veterans (CWVV) Program**
  (authorized under 38 U.S.C. § 1812)

A parent, guardian, or other authorized representative may submit this form
on behalf of a minor or incapacitated adult beneficiary.

**If you are not enrolled in one of these two programs, this form does not
apply to you.** For general VA health care travel reimbursement, visit
[VA Beneficiary Travel](https://www.va.gov/health-care/get-reimbursed-for-travel-pay/).

---

## What expenses you can claim

You can claim reimbursement for expenses related to covered medical
appointments, including:

- **Travel** — airline, bus, train, taxi, and other transportation
- **Privately owned vehicle (POV) mileage** — round-trip miles driven to
  the appointment (no receipt required for mileage)
- **Lodging** — hotel or other overnight accommodation
- **Meals** — food costs during travel
- **Other expenses** — parking, tolls, and similar incidentals

> **Important:** You must attach receipts for all expenses except POV
> mileage. Missing receipts will delay or prevent your reimbursement.

---

## What you'll need before you start

Gather the following before you begin. You'll need:

**For the patient (the child who received care):**
- Full legal name
- Social Security number
- Date of birth
- Current mailing address and phone number

**For the sponsor (the qualifying Veteran parent):**
- Full legal name
- Social Security number

**If you are claiming travel expenses:**
- Date of the medical appointment
- Provider Tax ID number (ask your provider's office)
- Provider's name (typed as electronic signature, as allowed by the form)
- Travel dates, departure and arrival cities and states, and travel times
- Mode of transportation used

**If an attendant traveled with the patient:**
- Attendant's full name and relationship to the patient

**If someone other than the patient is signing:**
- Representative's full legal name, Social Security number, mailing address,
  and phone number

**Receipts:**
- Photos (JPG or PNG) or PDF files of all receipts except POV mileage
- Maximum file size: 25 MB per file

## How to submit

You have two options:

### Option 1 — Submit online (preferred)

Sign in with Login.gov or ID.me to submit this form online. Signing in lets
us fill in some of your information automatically and gives you a confirmation
number when you're done.

[Sign in to start your claim](/health-care/file-10-7959e)

### Option 2 — Submit by mail or fax

Download and print [VA Form 10-7959E](https://www.va.gov/find-forms/about-form-10-7959e/).
Attach paper copies of your receipts. Send your completed form to:

**By mail:**
VHA Office of Integrated Veteran Care
P.O. Box 400
Spring City, PA 19475

**By fax:** 1-303-331-7807

---

## What happens after you submit

1. VHA's Office of Integrated Veteran Care (OIVC) will review your claim and
   verify your eligibility and the information you provided.
2. If OIVC needs more information, they will contact you at the phone number
   or address you provided.
3. If your claim is approved, reimbursement will be issued to the beneficiary
   (the patient).

**Questions about your claim?** Call OIVC at **1-833-930-0816**,
Monday–Friday, 8:05 a.m. – 6:30 p.m. ET.

## Privacy Act statement

Providing the information on this form is voluntary. However, failure to
provide the requested information may result in a delay or denial of
reimbursement. The information collected on this form is covered under
Privacy Act system of records 54VA16.

*OMB Respondent Burden Statement: Public reporting burden for this collection
of information is estimated to average 10 minutes per response, including the
time to review instructions, gather necessary data, and complete and review
the collection. OMB Control Number 2900-0219 expires 12/31/2027.*
